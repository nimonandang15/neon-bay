Neon Bay is an original open-world crime game built with three.js. Explore a neon-lit city on foot or by car, weave through traffic, dodge the police as your wanted level rises, and play through 6 story missions — with a HUD, minimap, and save support.

## What's in the city

- **Health + armor** — armor absorbs damage first; first-aid kits and vests scattered around the city; health regens when the heat is off.
- **Six weapons** — fists, baseball bat, pistol, shotgun, SMG, rifle, each with its own damage, range, fire rate and ammo. Switch with 1–6 or the mouse wheel; find the bigger guns as pickups.
- **Drive-by shooting** — fire the pistol or SMG from behind the wheel.
- **Six story jobs** — getaway, delivery, survival, takedown, timed escape, and a bodyguard job, all from fixer Marla Voss.
- **Money that matters** — jobs, taxi fares, hidden packages and bounties pay cash; spend it at Neon Arms & Snacks on ammo, armor and snacks.
- **Side work** — drive taxi fares, hunt 10 hidden packages, collect bounties on officers.
- **Three rides** — sedans, sports cars and trucks, each with different speed and toughness.
- **Day and night** — the sun sets, headlights come on, and rain rolls through.
- **A deeper wanted system** — roadblocks at 4–5 stars, and bribe markers that buy a star off.
- **Stats** — jobs done, cash earned, accuracy, takedowns and cars stolen on the pause screen.
- **Radio** — three synth stations while driving (Q to switch).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
